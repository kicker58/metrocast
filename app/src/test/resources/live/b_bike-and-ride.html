    Bike & Ride at Metro

    Your Bicycle Parking Made Easy

    Bike & Rides are free, secure bicycle facilities for you to store your bicycle as you ride our system. Only fully assembled, operable human-powered or electric-powered vehicles such as bicycles, e-bikes, and scooters are permitted. Gas-powered vehicles and vehicles not powered by human or electric energy are prohibited. The maximum permitted parking duration is 10 consecutive days, after which vehicles will be removed.

    Features

    • 24/7 access to card-controlled, sheltered bicycle parking, monitored by video surveillance.
    • Repair stands equipped with tools and air pumps.
    • Capacity for up to 100 bicycles per location.
    • Bright LED lighting and steel mesh walls.
    • Emergency call boxes.

    Locations operated by Metro

    How to Access Bike & Rides

    1. Register your Smart Trip card.*
    2. Sign up for free to use Metro operated Bike & Rides below. 
    3. Email a government issued ID to bike@wmata.com. Please allow three days to receive a confirmation email.
    4. Then, tap your Smart Trip card at any Bike & Ride entrance, lock your bicycle, and you're ready to ride Metro!

    * You must tap a physical Smart Trip card. Mobile Smart Trip does not work at Bike & Ride facilities.
