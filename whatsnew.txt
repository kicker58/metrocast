Live trains show the moment you open the app: they no longer wait for the weather, and arrivals are asked for first (before the weekly track layout and ride times).
If trains don't load on the first try (common right after your phone wakes up), MetroCast tries again within seconds, and as soon as your connection comes back.
The home screen says "Loading live times…" and "Loading where the trains are…" while trains load, instead of showing 0 trains.
Smoother: downloaded train data is kept in its own files, so saving a setting never waits on a refresh.
Sturdier: re-running the setup walkthrough from Settings can be cancelled with Back; a damaged saved trip or location is ignored instead of showing a blank name or breaking the weather; tapping the move arrows quickly in Settings no longer crashes; at most 200 trains are read from WMATA.
