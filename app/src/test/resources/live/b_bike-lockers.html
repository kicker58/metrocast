    Bike Lockers

    Bike locker information

    Smarter bike parking is rolling out at Metro

    Metro is continuing to improve our bicycle parking program. We are moving away from a keyed, annual rental system to a digital, keyless one making bike parking more flexible, affordable, and accessible. Lockers and smart racks will be accessible via an app; the smart racks are free to rent, while lockers will be available to rent on-demand by the hour for 5 cents an hour up to $1 per day.

    This will make parking your bike more secure and convenient. Across our system, we're investing in new facilities to better serve you: 450 new standard bike lockers, 50 new oversized lockers, 100 new smart, self-locking racks, 75 new repair stations, and 600 new u-racks.

    New bike parking amenities have been installed at select stations and new bike equipment will continue to be added at stations across the system in phases. The equipment will be functional once it is installed. Amenities will differ per station.

    Frequently asked questions

    • Download the BikeLink app — available on Apple Store and Google Play.
    • Create a BikeLink account and determine if you would like to use a smart rack or a locker.
    • Search the real-time app for locker and smart rack availability, rates, and related information.
    • Follow the on-screen instructions.
    • Open the locker or smart rack to park your bike.

    For assistance, please contact the BikeLink Support Line at 888-540-0546 (24 hours/day, 365 days/year).

    • The bike lockers are five cents an hour up to $1.00 a day.
    • Smart racks are free to reserve through the app. A credit card is required for verification only.
    • Park your bike for a maximum of 5 days at a time.

    Bike lockers and smart racks do not have Tap. Ride. Go. contactless payment, or “Smart Trip” tap functionality.

    Payments and reservations must be made via BikeLink’s app or by contacting BikeLink’s customer support (888) 540-0546 (24 hours per day, 365 days per year).

    Our system will feature three locker types: standard single, standard double-sided, and oversized. The locker type varies by station.

    • Standard Single Locker: Internal dimensions are 77” L × 31” W × 52” H.
    • Standard Double-Sided Locker: Same internal dimensions as the single but divided in the middle to accommodate two bicycles accessible from doors on opposite sides.
    • Oversized Locker: Internal dimensions are 120” L × 43” W × 52” H, designed to fit cargo bikes such as the Bunch Bike, RadWagon, Urban Arrow, and similar models.

    • Open the app, select a rack, and unlock the arm
    • Put the arm through the bike frame
    • Pull the chain through the wheel
    • Ensure that the chain loop is secured to the arm
    • Push arm closed until ‘click’

    Need more assistance?

    For any questions regarding our bike parking program, please email us at bike@wmata.com.

    Issues with the locker?

    For any questions regarding locker rental or access, please contact BikeLink at (888) 540-0546 (24 hours/day, 365 days/year).

    You can also email support@bikelink.org or visit the BikeLink FAQ for more information.
